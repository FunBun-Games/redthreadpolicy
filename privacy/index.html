Who we are

Red Thread is published by Kostiantyn Sherman, an individual entrepreneur registered in Ukraine (9 Macheretivska St, Chernihiv 14000, Ukraine). For this policy Kostiantyn Sherman is the data controller. Contact: funbun.dev@gmail.com.

The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or contacts. Nothing is sent to us. The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone. It asks to add pictures to Photos only when you save a result card.

Tracking and ad personalisation

On iOS, the game asks for permission before using your advertising identifier. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.

The game is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content suitable for teenagers (Google's “T” rating), and ads are not treated as child-directed.

Third parties

We use these services, which process data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — how Google uses data from partner apps
  • Google Firebase — Firebase privacy and security
  • Apple Game Center and App Store purchases — Apple Privacy Policy. Game Center is controlled by Apple. You can sign out in iOS Settings → Game Center.

We do not sell your personal data. Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you delete it (see below) or delete the game.
  • Analytics (Firebase): up to 14 months, then it is deleted automatically.
  • Advertising data and Game Center data: as set by Google and Apple in their policies.

Your rights and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA, the UK GDPR, or Ukrainian law), you may ask us to access, correct or delete data about you, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable form, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to your data protection authority.

  • Delete data in the game: Settings → Delete my data. This removes your progress, coins, outfits and settings from the device and resets the analytics identifier, so later events can no longer be linked to earlier ones. Purchases are kept by Apple and can be restored for free.
  • Data already sent to Google Analytics cannot be linked to you without your device's analytics identifier. If you still want it deleted, write to funbun.dev@gmail.com; we will explain how to find your app instance ID on your device and delete the data linked to it.
  • Withdraw advertising consent: Settings → Ad privacy choices and iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Deleting the game removes everything stored on your device.

We answer within 30 days.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Відстеження й персоналізація реклами

На iOS гра питає дозволу, перш ніж використати ваш рекламний ідентифікатор. Якщо ви відмовите, реклама все одно показується, але не персоналізована. Змінити вибір можна будь-коли в Параметрах iOS → Приватність і безпека → Відстеження.

Якщо ви в Європейській економічній зоні, Великій Британії чи Швейцарії, гра показує форму згоди Google перед персоналізованою рекламою. Змінити вибір можна будь-коли в грі: Налаштування → «Згода на рекламу».

Гра не призначена для дітей. Реклама обмежена контентом, придатним для підлітків (рейтинг Google «T»), і не вважається дитячою.
